Interactive Onboarding: Know Your Rights & System Training

Welcome to your Recovery-Ready Workplace onboarding! Under federal and state frameworks, you are entitled to a psychologically safe, supportive work environment. This module teaches you how, where, when, and why to utilize this portal.

1. THE "HOW": Zero-Retaliation Confidentiality

When you submit a request for assistance or housing/recovery navigation through this portal, your data routes directly to certified external Network Liaisons (CNLs). It bypasses internal HR disciplinary pipelines entirely. Sharing your confirmation token with management is strictly your choice.

2. THE "WHERE": Accessing Multi-Category Support

You can access this portal 24/7 from any device. Whether you need help with vehicle repair, rental navigation, substance use support, or schedule adjustments, everything is consolidated in one secure gateway.

3. THE "WHEN": Early Intervention & Check-Ins

Use the check-in form whenever you feel mounting stress, life disruptions, or burnout—long before it affects your employment. If you ever decide to leave or quit, our exit feedback tool ensures your voice helps shape a better workplace for others.

4. THE "WHY": Fostering a Recovery-Ready Culture

We believe that stability breeds excellence. By removing outside barriers (housing, transit, health), we protect your well-being and build a truly resilient workforce.
